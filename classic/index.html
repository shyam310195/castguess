How to play

  1. Act I · The faces. Three actors from one film.
  2. Act II · One more face. A fourth actor steps in.
  3. Act III · The names. Everyone's name appears.
  4. Act IV · The plot. A short synopsis, names hidden.

A new act starts every 15 seconds (you can make it 30 seconds or 1 minute in Settings). Guess any time. Earlier acts are worth more (100, 75, 50, 25) plus a speed bonus. You get 3 guesses per film; each miss costs 10 points. Correct answers in a row raise your streak multiplier. Miss a film and you lose a star. Lose all 3 and the curtain falls. Don't know a film? Skip it: up to 3 skips per game, no star lost, but your streak resets.

Hands-free voice

While a film is on screen, the microphone listens. Say the film's name and it counts the moment it's right. Chatter and wrong titles are ignored and never cost you anything. You can also say “next clue” or “skip”.

Your browser's speech service (Apple or Google) turns speech into text. Your browser will ask for microphone permission once. The mic is only on during a film, never on menus.
