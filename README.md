# Guess Night

A free, phone-first guessing game for solo play or party nights. Four clues per round across nine categories: **Movies**, **TV shows**, **Actors**, **Famous people**, **Sports stars**, **Countries**, **Cities & landmarks**, **Animals** and **Brands**. Mix them for a party, pick 5 to 30 rounds or play endless, and guess by typing or out loud.

**Play:** https://shyam310195.github.io/castguess/

The original movies-only game, CastGuess, is still available at https://shyam310195.github.io/castguess/classic/
