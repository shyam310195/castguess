How to play

    A new clue arrives every 15 seconds (30 seconds or 1 minute in Settings). Guess any time. Earlier clues are worth more (100, 75, 50, 25) plus a speed bonus. You get 3 guesses per round; each miss costs 10 points. Right answers in a row raise your streak multiplier. Miss one and you lose a star; lose all 3 and the curtain falls. Skip up to 3 per game without losing a star (your streak resets). A close answer, like another film in the same series, earns 75%.

    Hands-free voice

    While a round is on screen, the microphone listens. Say the answer and it counts the moment it's right. Chatter and wrong answers are ignored and never cost you anything. You can also say “next clue” or “skip”.

    Your browser's speech service (Apple or Google) turns speech into text. Your browser will ask for microphone permission once. The mic is only on during a round, never on menus.
